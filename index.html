What is AI?

AI stands for artificial intelligence. It is a computer program that has learned from a huge amount of writing and pictures.

Think of a very well-read helper. You ask a question in everyday words. It writes an answer. It does not "know" things like a person does. It predicts a good reply from what it has seen.

What can AI do for you?

  • Write an email. Tell it who it is for and what you need.
  • Plan your week. Give it your tasks and it suggests an order.
  • Explain a bill or a form. Ask it to say it in simple words.
  • Make a shopping list. Say what meals you plan to cook.
  • Practise a language. Ask it to chat with you slowly.

How to start

  1. Open a free AI chat tool from a company you trust.
  2. Type what you want in plain words, like you would tell a friend.
  3. Read the answer. Ask a follow-up if something is unclear.

Things to watch out for

  • It can be wrong. It sometimes sounds sure when it is not. Check anything important.
  • Protect your privacy. Never share passwords, bank details or ID numbers.
  • Keep your judgment. Use AI as a helper, not as the final decision-maker.

Questions people ask

Is AI free to use?

Many tools have a free plan with limits. Paid plans usually give more use. Check each tool's own page.

Will AI take my job?

Nobody can say for sure. A good habit is to learn how to use it, so it helps you rather than surprises you.

Is it safe?

It is safe if you are careful. Do not share private details, and check important answers.

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can send a text message, you can use an AI chat.

Which tool should I pick?

Start with one from a well-known company. Try it for a week before you decide.
