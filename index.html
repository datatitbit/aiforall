The essentials of AI, explained simply.

Six short parts. By the end you will know the core basics: what AI is, what it can do, how to ask it well, and what to watch out for.

1 What is AI?

AI (artificial intelligence) is a computer program that learned patterns from a huge amount of text, pictures, sound and data. You give it an input. It predicts a useful output.

Think of a very well-read helper. It does not "know" things like a person does. It makes a smart guess, and that is why you still check the result.

You ask→ AI predicts→ You check

2 Common types of AI

The easiest way to sort AI is by what goes in and what comes out.

Text

Reads and writes words. Answers, summaries, emails, translations.

Image

Makes or edits pictures from a short description (text to image).

Video

Makes short clips from a description or a picture (text to video).

Audio

Speech to text, text to speech, voices and music.

Prediction

Spots patterns in data. Suggests songs, flags fraud, forecasts sales.

Agents

Do steps for you, like search, write and send, with your approval.

3 Common everyday uses

  • Write and polish emails
  • Summarise long documents
  • Explain a bill or form
  • Plan your week or a trip
  • Learn a language
  • Brainstorm ideas
  • Make images for posts
  • Turn a voice note into text

4 Prompting: how to ask well

A prompt is what you type to an AI. Clear prompts get better answers. A simple framework to remember is R-T-C-F:

R: Role

Who should it act as?

T: Task

What exactly do you want done?

C: Context

Who is it for? What should it know?

F: Format

Length, tone, list or paragraph?

Weak

Write about healthy eating.

Strong

You are a friendly nutritionist. Write a 5-point guide to healthy eating for busy office workers. Keep it simple, with no jargon, and end with one tip I can try today.

  • Give an example of what a good answer looks like.
  • Ask for steps when the task is big. "First outline, then write."
  • Improve it. Reply "shorter", "simpler" or "more formal" until it fits.
  • Start a fresh chat for a new topic.

5 Pitfalls to avoid

It makes things up

It can sound sure and be wrong. Check facts, numbers and names.

Privacy

Never share passwords, bank details or ID numbers.

Bias

It learned from human writing, so it can repeat unfair views.

Ownership

Check the tool's rules before you use AI work commercially.

Fakes and scams

AI can fake voices, photos and messages. Verify before you trust.

Over-reliance

Use it as a helper. You make the final decision.

6 Next step: automation and agents

AI becomes more useful when it is connected to your other tools. An automation is a chain of steps that runs by itself. An AI agent is an AI step that can decide what to do next, and you stay in charge by approving the important actions.

Trigger→ AI step→ Action

Example: a new email arrives (trigger), AI summarises it (AI step), the summary is saved to a sheet (action).

The 5 basics to remember

  1. AI predicts. It does not truly know.
  2. Pick the type that matches your input and output.
  3. Be clear: role, task, context, format.
  4. Check anything important before you use it.
  5. Keep private details out, and keep the final say.
